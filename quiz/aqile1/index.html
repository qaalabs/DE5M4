    Exam: Agile Framework

    Which Agile framework is the most useful in all situations?

    • Scrum
    • Kanban
    • DSDM
    • It depends

    Not every Agile framework will be right for every team. You need to experiment and do research into different frameworks to make sure you are using the one that is best for you and your team.

    Learn more: https://app.qa.com/course/introduction-to-agile-and-scrum-productowner/agile-frameworks/

    What is currently the number one reason for adopting Agile?

    • To accelerate software development
    • To make teams more productive
    • To make teams leaner and able to be reactive to the market
    • To be enable teams to quickly develop a product, regardless of quality

    While there are many diverse reasons for adopting Agile, the number one reason right now is to accelerate software development.

    Stacey's process complexity model shows us that Agile thinking is best _______________. (Choose two answers)

    • When there is less understanding of the requirements or the solution
    • When there is high certainty
    • When there is high complexity
    • When there is low certainty and low complexity

    According to the Stacey’s process complexity model, an Agile approach is best suited for high uncertainty/complexity. A low understanding of requirements or the solution leads to high uncertainty and potentially high complexity.

    Adopting an Agile mindset is beneficial for all circumstances.

    Learn more: https://app.qa.com/course/agile-fundamentals-module-1/when-agile/

    Agile is a ______________.

    • Mindset
    • Framework
    • Methodology
    • System

    Agile is a mindset. While there are many Agile frameworks and methodologies, it really comes down to a way of thinking.

    Learn more: https://app.qa.com/course/introduction-to-agile-and-scrum-productowner/what-is-agile/

    Which Agile principles are the most important?

    • Principles 1, 2, 3 & 4
    • Principles 5, 6, 7 & 8
    • Principles 9, 10, 11 & 12
    • All Agile principles are equally important

    Every principle is equally important. To work in an Agile way, you need to embrace all 12.

    Learn more: https://app.qa.com/course/introduction-to-agile-and-scrum-productowner/principles-of-agile/

    Which Agile Frameworks focus on the Product Delivery?

    • Scrum and PRINCE2
    • Kanban and Scrum
    • AgilePM, DSDM, and PRINCE2
    • AgilePM, DSDM, and Scrum

    Frameworks like AgilePM DSDM and PRINCE2 Agile take into account the entire project lifecycle, while Scrum and Kanban focus on product delivery.

    Learn more: https://app.qa.com/course/introduction-to-agile-and-scrum-productowner/agile-frameworks/

    Aside from adopting the Agile principles and values, what else do you need to work in an Agile way?

    • Software to track progress
    • Management enthusiasm
    • A Scrum Master
    • None of these answers are correct

    Agile is a mindset. You don’t need any of the frameworks to work in an Agile way, if you take on and use the Agile principles and values in your day to day work.
