    Quiz: Agile

    What does the Agile Manifesto say about responding to change?

    • Changes are an expected part of the development process and agile teams should have the ability to adapt.
    • Changes to the plan can be made as long as the development team approves the change.
    • Changes can be made once a project manager updates the project plan.
    • The product owner is the only person who can approve plan changes.

    Explanation

    The Agile Manifesto says that agile teams value responding to change over following a plan. This means that agile teams should expect and value change, even if it is late in the development process. Mature agile teams are prepared for and can adapt to change.

    The manifesto does not say anything about how changes are handled, only that there is value in responding to change.

    Learn more: https://agilemanifesto.org/

    Which of the following statements are considered to be part of the Agile Manifesto? (Choose 2 answers)

    • Deliver working software frequently, from a couple of weeks to a couple of months, with a preference to the shorter timescale.
    • Changes to requirements late in the development cycle are discouraged to ensure product stability before release.
    • Comprehensive design documentation delivered before development work begins will enhance product quality.
    • The best architectures, requirements, and designs emerge from self-organizing teams

    Explanation

    The Agile Manifesto is a document often referenced when discussing Agile principles. From this manifesto came 12 guiding principles for teams implementing and practicing agile processes.

    Of the choices provided, two answers are among these 12 guiding principles:

    1. Deliver working software frequently, from a couple of weeks to a couple of months, with a preference to the shorter timescale.
    2. The best architectures, requirements, and designs emerge from self-organizing teams.

    The other choices listed are not found among the 12 principles and are counter to the ideas expressed in the Agile Manifesto.

    Learn more: http://agilemanifesto.org/principles.html

    A development team working on maintenance and minor feature requests for an existing application has decided to begin practicing an agile development process. This team needs to continuously release the product updates as work is completed, and they wish to maximize the flow of their completed work.

    Which agile framework is best suited for this team?

    • Kanban
    • Waterfall
    • Extreme programming (XP)
    • Scrum

    Explanation

    Kanban is a good choice for teams that need to release their product or service as work is completed rather than following a particular cadence associated with practicing scrum. Another advantage of Kanban is that it is beneficial for teams that wish to maximize the flow of work completed.

    A waterfall software development process is not considered an agile framework.

    Learn more: https://www.atlassian.com/agile/kanban

    Which of the following statements about Scrum are correct? (Choose 2 answers)

    • Scrum should only be used to manage simple projects
    • Scrum is an iterative process framework
    • Scrum defines prescriptive processes for managing project work
    • Scrum is well suited to manage work on complex projects

    Explanation

    While scrum can be used to manage simple projects, it is particularly well suited to handle complex projects with a non-trivial amount of work to complete. Scrum does not define a prescriptive step-by-step process for managing project work. Instead, scrum is an empirical process where plans evolve based on observations rather than following a theoretical plan. A core concept in scrum is an iteration or sprint. A sprint is a time block that is typically 2 - 4 weeks when a set number of work items are completed.

    Learn more: https://app.qa.com/resource/history-of-agile

    Which of the following statements is in line with an agile mindset?

    • The best measure of progress is the number of work items completed by the end of each iteration.
    • The best designs emerge when product owners provide technical guidance in product backlog work items.
    • A team's primary focus should be meeting customer needs, even if it means adapting to changing requirements.
    • When faced with uncertainty, work should not start until product requirements are clearly documented.

    Explanation

    The statement that is most in line with an agile mindset is:

    A team's primary focus should be meeting customer needs, even if it means adapting to changing requirements.

    The other statements are not consistent with the Agile Manifesto and not in line with the 12 Principles of agile.

    • The best measure of progress is the number of work items completed by the end of each iteration. --This statement is incorrect because the number of work items is not a perfect measure of progress, particularly if those work items do not meet customer expectations or needs. The best measure of progress is measured by working software that meets customer needs.

    • When faced with uncertainty, work should not start until product requirements are clearly documented. -- This statement is incorrect because even when there is uncertainty, it is best to try something and then solicit feedback from stakeholders and customers. Waiting for complete clarity is counter to agile's idea of iterative development.

    • The best designs emerge when product owners provide technical guidance in product backlog work items. -- This statement is incorrect because the best designs emerge when development teams are given the freedom to self organize and collaborate to find solutions. Technical solutions or information about how a work item should be implemented should not be included in product backlog work items.

    Learn more: https://www.agilealliance.org/agile101/12-principles-behind-the-agile-manifesto/
