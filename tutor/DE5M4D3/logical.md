## <mark>⚒️ Groups evolve conceptual model into a logical model</mark>

### Instructions

- Schema design decisions
- Performance considerations
- Evolution planning

### Report back

- Each person in the group will report back on one design decision the group has made.

---

### Aim to answer the following:

**What was the decision?**

- Clearly name the design choice you made

**Why did you make it?**

- What specific requirement, constraint, or goal led to this choice

**What would this achieve?**

- Explain the benefit or trade-off
- Could this choice create problems later
