## Scrum Artefacts

**Slides: slides/artefact.md**

- Agile itself defines no artefacts - Scrum is one Agile framework, and the Scrum Guide defines three: Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, and Increment
- Epics and User Stories are common practice (from XP), not Scrum Guide artefacts - they live inside the Product Backlog and help us organise and explain work
- Each artefact has a commitment: Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done
- The 2020 Scrum Guide says "Developers", not "Development Team"
