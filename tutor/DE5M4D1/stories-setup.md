## Write User Stories in groups

Now we're going to break our Epics down into User Stories.

**We'll review some examples together afterwards.**

```text
## Write User Stories

### For each requirement, ask yourself:
1. Who is the user?
2. What do they want to do?
3. Why do they want it?

Working now in groups, share the Epics you created. Then:

- Go through your requirements under that Epic
- Convert each requirement into one or more user stories
- Write down the user story in the correct format.
```
