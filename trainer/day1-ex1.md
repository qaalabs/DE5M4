# Opening Exercise: Designing Data-Driven Digital Products

## Exercise Setup (10 minutes)

**Introduction Script:**
Welcome to Module 4: Planning a Data Engineering Product. Over the next four days, we'll explore the comprehensive process of designing data solutions that deliver real business value.

But before we dive into frameworks and methodologies, I want to start with something you all have experience with. You've all used digital products. But have you ever stopped to think: **How are they designed?** What goes into that process—from idea to something people use every day?

In your groups, I want you to imagine you're working for a company that wants to create a **new data-driven digital product.** Your task is to think through: **What steps would you take to design it?**

There are no wrong answers here — I want you to draw on your collective experience and knowledge. Think about both the business and technical aspects of design. What information would you need? Who would you involve? What would your process look like?

**Group Formation:**

- Form groups of 3-4 people in breakout rooms
- Provide a collaborative workspace (virtual whiteboard like Miro or Google Jamboard)

## Exercise Structure (30-35 minutes total)

### Part 1: Brainstorming Design Steps (15 minutes)

Ask each group to create a visual representation of their design process, addressing:

- What are the key phases or stages in designing a data product?
- What activities happen in each phase?
- Who needs to be involved at each stage?
- What inputs and outputs would each stage have?

**Instructions to participants:**

Use your virtual whiteboard to map out the design process as a series of steps. For each step, note:

- The name of the step
- Key activities involved
- People/roles who should participate
- Information needed for this step
- Outputs produced at this step

Be prepared to present your process in 2-3 minutes.

### Part 2: Apply to a Scenario (10 minutes)
Give all groups the same brief scenario to apply their process to:

**Scenario:**
A retail company with 200 physical stores and an e-commerce website wants to create a data product that helps them understand customer behavior across both channels to improve inventory management and personalization. They have point-of-sale data, online transaction records, customer profiles, and inventory data available.

**Task:**
Using the design process your group created, quickly outline how you would approach this specific project. What would be your first three steps, and what challenges do you anticipate?

### Part 3: Group Presentations (10 minutes)

- Each group briefly presents their design process and initial approach to the scenario
- Limit to 2-3 minutes per group
- Focus on differences in approaches between groups

## Debrief and Connection (10 minutes)

After all presentations, facilitate a discussion highlighting:

- Common elements across different approaches
- Unique insights from specific groups
- How these approaches connect to industry standards

**Debrief Questions:**

- "What similarities did you notice across all the approaches?"
- "What were some unique perspectives that stood out?"
- "How might the design process differ for data products compared to other digital products?"
- "Which parts of the process seemed most challenging to define?"

## Bridge to Module Content (5 minutes)

Connect the exercise directly to the module journey:
What's fascinating is how many of the elements you've identified align with industry best practices for data product planning. Throughout this module, we'll explore structured approaches to each of these phases:

- Today, we'll focus on Agile methodologies that help translate business needs into actionable plans
- Tomorrow, we'll explore the technical architecture that supports data product development
- Day 3 will cover data storage and processing approaches
- By Day 4, you'll bring everything together in a comprehensive data product plan

What we've started here will serve as a foundation for our entire journey through this module.
