    Breakout Activity: How are Digital Products Designed?

    In groups brainstorm the digital design process, addressing:

    • What are 3 or 4 key phases or stages in designing a data product?
    • What activities happen in each phase?
    • Who needs to be involved at each stage?
    • What inputs and outputs would each stage have?

    Instructions

    Map out the design process as a series of steps.

    For each step, note:

    • The name of the phase
    • Key activities involved
    • People/roles who should participate
    • Information needed for this step
    • Outputs produced at this step

    Group sharing

    Be prepared to present your process for 2-3 minutes.
