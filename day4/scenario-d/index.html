    Day 4 Planning Scenario – Variant 4

    Sensitive Data, Security & Governance Data Product

    Organisational context

    An organisation processes data that includes personally identifiable and sensitive information as part of its core operations. Leadership wants improved reporting to support operational oversight while ensuring that data is handled responsibly and in line with governance expectations.

    At present:

    • sensitive and non-sensitive data are often handled together
    • access controls vary between teams
    • reporting processes rely heavily on manual steps

    The organisation has committed to:

    • strengthening data governance and security practices
    • reducing risk while maintaining operational efficiency
    • aligning data platform decisions with sustainability and compliance objectives

    Business need

    The organisation requires a secure and well-governed data product to support operational reporting.

    The data product should:

    • support regular reporting for authorised users
    • protect sensitive data fields appropriately
    • provide clear auditability of access and changes
    • remain efficient and maintainable over time

    There is interest in expanding access in the future, provided governance controls are in place.

    Data sources

    Data is sourced from:

    • an internal system containing sensitive personal data
    • supplementary reference data from internal teams

    Data volumes are moderate but expected to grow steadily.

    Constraints and considerations

    • Access to sensitive data must be role-based
    • Some data must be retained for defined periods
    • The solution must support audit and review activities
    • Overly complex security solutions are discouraged
    • Cloud solutions are acceptable if governance requirements are met

    Known challenges (intentionally open)

    • Balancing access needs with data protection
    • Avoiding duplication of secured datasets
    • Preventing governance controls from becoming a maintenance burden

    Your role

    You are part of the data engineering team responsible for planning the data product.

    You are expected to:

    • collate and refine business requirements
    • propose a high-level design
    • justify decisions around security and access
    • consider sustainability, cost, governance, and long-term maintenance

    No implementation is required.
