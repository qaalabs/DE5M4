    Day 4 ~ Agenda

    Planning sustainable data products through requirements and design decisions

    9:30–9:40 - Welcome (10 mins)

    Purpose

    • Set expectations
    • Position the day as engineering thinking, not report writing

    Key messages

    • This is about how you plan and justify decisions
    • Sustainability = efficiency, longevity, reduced waste
    • Everything today links to real workplace projects

    No activity here - just framing.

    9:40–10:40 - Session 1 (60 mins)

    From messy needs to refined requirements

    Focus

    • Collating
    • Evaluating
    • Refining

    Activity (core of the session)

    • Groups receive an unstructured business scenario
    • They identify stakeholders and extract requirements
    • They refine and prioritise what actually matters

    Output

    • Short list of refined requirements with rationale

    Sustainability angle

    • Which requirements were removed or simplified
    • Why less can be more

    EPA alignment

    • K12 - collated and evaluated business requirements
    • S2 - refined requirements to support system design

    11:00–12:10 - Session 2 (70 mins)

    Design options and trade-offs

    Focus

    • Choosing between technical approaches
    • Rejecting alternatives

    Activity

    • Present 4–6 design choices relevant to the scenario
    • Groups must choose one option per decision point
    • They must reject at least one option and justify it

    Output

    • Agreed high-level design with decision rationale

    Sustainability angle

    • Efficiency over complexity
    • Avoiding unnecessary compute, storage, and movement

    EPA alignment

    • S2 - design decisions
    • K7 - selecting sustainable solutions

    13:20–14:30 - Session 3 (70 mins)

    Lifecycle thinking and long-term sustainability

    Focus

    • Build vs run vs maintain
    • Where systems become inefficient over time

    Activity

    • Groups sketch or review their pipeline
    • Annotate where costs and inefficiencies could grow
    • Identify at least two improvements

    Output

    • Annotated system view highlighting risks and improvements

    Sustainability angle

    • Long-term efficiency
    • Maintainability as a sustainability strategy

    EPA alignment

    • S2 - building and maintaining systems
    • Net-zero considerations through longevity

    14:50–15:50 - Session 4 (60 mins)

    Explaining decisions clearly

    Focus

    • Articulation, not writing
    • Making sustainability explicit

    Activity

    • Each group selects one key decision
    • They explain it verbally using guided prompts

    Prompts

    • What was the requirement?
    • What options existed?
    • What did you choose?
    • Why is it more sustainable?

    Output

    • Clear, assessor-friendly explanations

    EPA alignment

    • K12, S2, K7, S27 - explanation and justification

    15:50–16:00 - Wrap-up (10 mins)

    Purpose

    • Reinforce transfer to workplace projects

    Reflection prompts

    • Where could you apply this thinking in your current role?
    • What decision could you justify more clearly tomorrow?

    No writing required.

    Why this fits your constraints

    • One coherent scenario across the day
    • Each session has a distinct purpose
    • Sustainability is embedded, not bolted on
    • Zero EPA report drafting

    If you want, next we can:

    • design one reusable scenario you can run every cohort
    • create facilitator prompts only (no learner handouts)
    • map each session explicitly to Module 4 outcomes
