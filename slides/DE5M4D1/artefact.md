## Agile Artefacts
Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, and Increment are the core Agile artefacts, while Epics and User Stories are backlog artefacts that help us organise and explain work.

**Epic**: An epic is a large piece of work that is too big to complete in a single sprint.

**User Stories**: A user story is a short description of a requirement written from the perspective of the user or stakeholder.

**Product Backlog**: The Product Backlog is an ordered list of everything that might be needed in the product.

**Sprint Backlog**: The Sprint Backlog is the team’s plan for the current sprint.

**Increment**: The Increment is the sum of all completed work at the end of a sprint, integrated with everything built before.
