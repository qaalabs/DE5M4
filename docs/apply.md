# Apply: Planning a Data Engineering Product

In this workplace activity, you are tasked with completing the following activities whilst documenting them in a report. The entire report should include insights and findings from each task you complete, along with a comprehensive overview of relevant practices within your organisation. 

Throughout your report, demonstrate how you used initiative and innovation to problem solve and troubleshoot, providing creative solutions to the tasks you completed. Highlight specific instances where your approach was particularly innovative or effective.

*To effectively accomplish this activity, complete the steps/tasks below.*

## Task 1: Explore data use cases (S12)
Research and summarise three current data use cases within your organisation, highlighting how each supports operational objectives.

You are required to explore and summarise three current data use cases within your organisation. These use cases should illustrate how data supports the organisation’s operational objectives and helps improve decision-making, efficiency, and overall business performance.

You will need to collaborate with different departments or teams to gather insights on how data is being used. Examples might include data-driven customer insights, predictive maintenance using machine learning, or real-time analytics for process optimisation. Your goal is to provide a clear link between these data applications and their contribution to the organisation's goals.

## Task 2: Identify and propose solutions for a data provision problem (S11, S14, S25)
Engage with internal customers, key stakeholders, and users to identify a data provision problem within your organisation. (S11)

Describe the identified problem in detail and analyse the impact of this problem on the organisation. (S11)

Develop three high-level solutions to address the problem. Assess the risks and benefits of each proposed solution, potential operating benefits, and environmental impact. Use the SDLC when developing your solutions. (S11)

Evaluate existing tools and technology to determine how they can be maximised, developed, or improved. (S25)

Conduct research to identify new tools and technologies for your proposed solution, evaluate their pros and cons, and consider the costs and implementation implications. (S14)

This task aims to foster critical thinking in identifying and solving data-related challenges.

You will need to engage with internal customers and stakeholders to identify a data provision problem, analyse its impact on operations, and propose a high-level solution.

## Task 3: Analyse your organisation’s business continuity policies (K21) 
Analyse and summarise your organisation’s existing policies and procedures for business continuity planning.

Identify specific measures in place to ensure uninterrupted data provision.

Describe how your organisation communicates downtime and issues with database access, in line with the policies, in order to mitigate operational impact whilst ensuring business continuity. ​

Evaluate the potential impact on the organisation if data provision is disrupted.

Examine how your organisation’s continuity planning process aligns with your upcoming project or data product.

If your organisation does not have a business continuity plan in place:

- design a draft business continuity plan.

- discuss the draft plan with key stakeholders.

This task focuses on understanding and analysing strategies related to data provision. You will examine your organisation's current policies, identify continuity measures, and assess the impact of any disruptions to data services on the organisation.

## Task 4: Root cause analysis (K23)
Research and document your organisation’s approach to analysis and root cause investigation for existing data products, including failure or incident responses.

From that research, create a risk matrix outlining reactive and proactive measures, with considerations for additional necessary elements.
