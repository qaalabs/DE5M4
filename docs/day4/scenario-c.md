# Day 4 Planning Scenario C

## High-Volume Operational Data & Cost-Efficiency Data Product

### Organisational context

A growing commercial organisation provides digital services to a large and expanding customer base. Leadership wants better visibility of **operational activity volumes** to support capacity planning, cost control, and service optimisation.

Currently:

* operational data is spread across multiple systems
* reports are slow to produce and difficult to reconcile
* teams regularly recreate similar datasets for different purposes

The organisation has set internal targets to:

* reduce operational costs
* avoid unnecessary data duplication
* align data platform decisions with sustainability objectives

### Business need

The organisation requires a **scalable data product** that supports operational and cost-efficiency reporting.

The data product should:

* support daily operational reporting
* enable analysis of trends as volumes increase
* reduce duplication of datasets and logic
* remain cost-effective as usage grows

There is interest in future advanced analytics, but this is not an immediate requirement.

### Data sources

Data is sourced from:

* multiple high-volume transactional systems
* event-style data generated by user activity

Data volumes are already significant and expected to increase rapidly.

### Constraints and considerations

* Real-time reporting is **not required**, but data latency must be predictable
* Storage and compute costs are a known concern
* The solution must scale without significant re-engineering
* Access to detailed data should be limited to relevant teams
* Cloud-based solutions are preferred for scalability reasons

### Known challenges (intentionally open)

* Data volumes may grow faster than expected
* Stakeholders are concerned about ongoing platform costs
* There is pressure to “design once and reuse” rather than rebuild later

## Your role

You are part of the data engineering team responsible for planning the data product.

You are expected to:

* collate and refine business requirements
* propose a high-level design
* justify key architectural decisions
* consider sustainability, cost, and long-term scalability

No implementation is required.
