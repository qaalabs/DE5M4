## Why these scenarios work well

This scenario naturally enables:

* requirement refinement (what is really needed?)
* trade-offs (batch vs real-time, duplication vs reuse)
* sustainability discussion (efficiency, reduction of waste)
* compliance awareness (retention, access control)
* cost and scaling considerations

And crucially:

* there is **no single correct answer**
