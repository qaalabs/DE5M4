# Day 4: How today works

## Purpose of today

Today is about **planning and decision-making**, not building or writing reports.

You will practise how data engineers think about requirements, design choices, and sustainability.

## How we’ll work

* You’ll work in the same group all day
* Each group has a different organisational scenario
* The activities are the same for everyone

There is **no single correct solution**.

## What will change during the day

* The **questions** will change each session
* The **scenario** stays the same
* Your design will evolve as you make decisions

## What I expect from you

* Make choices, not lists of options
* Be able to explain *why* you chose something
* Consider cost, sustainability, and long-term impact
* Be comfortable with ambiguity

## What we are not doing

* No coding
* No tools or calculators
* No report writing

## How this helps you

The thinking you practise today:

* mirrors real workplace planning
* supports later modules
* builds confidence in explaining decisions
