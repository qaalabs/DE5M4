# Day 4 Planning Scenario B

## Service Performance & Compliance Reporting Data Product

### Organisational context

A public-facing organisation delivers services to customers through a mix of digital platforms and back-office systems. Senior leaders want clearer insight into **service performance**, including demand levels, turnaround times, and service outcomes.

At present:

* reporting is created manually by different teams
* figures are often challenged due to inconsistent definitions
* historical data is difficult to analyse

The organisation has a stated objective to:

* improve operational efficiency
* reduce unnecessary reprocessing and rework
* ensure technology decisions align with sustainability and governance expectations

### Business need

The organisation requires a **central data product** to support performance and compliance reporting.

The data product should:

* support regular performance reporting for management
* enable trend analysis over time
* reduce manual report creation
* be reliable, auditable, and maintainable

The data product may later be reused to support service improvement initiatives.

### Data sources

Data is sourced from:

* an operational case management system
* periodic extracts from a third-party service provider

Data volumes are moderate now but expected to grow as service usage increases.

### Constraints and considerations

* Reporting is required on a **daily** basis
* Historical data must be retained to support audits and reviews
* Some data fields are sensitive and require controlled access
* The organisation operates under regulatory oversight
* Costs must be controlled and justified over the long term
* Cloud solutions are permitted but must meet governance requirements

## Known challenges (intentionally open)

* Stakeholders disagree on which performance metrics matter most
* Some reports are required for compliance, others for operational insight
* There is concern about building a solution that is costly to run or difficult to change

## Your role

You are part of the data engineering team responsible for planning the data product.

You are expected to:

* collate and refine business requirements
* propose a high-level design
* justify key technical decisions
* consider sustainability, cost, compliance, and long-term operation
