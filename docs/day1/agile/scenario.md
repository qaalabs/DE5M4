# Activity: Apply your design steps to a scenario

## [Scenario A - Retail Company](scenario-a.md)

A retail company with 200 physical stores and an e-commerce website wants to create a data product that **helps them understand customer behaviour** across both channels to improve inventory management and personalisation. They have point-of-sale data, online transaction records, customer profiles, and inventory data available.

## [Scenario B - Healthcare Provider](scenario-b.md)

A regional healthcare network with 5 hospitals and 20 clinics wants to create a data product that **predicts patient readmission risk** within 30 days of discharge. They have electronic health records, medication data, demographic information, and historical admission records available, but data is currently siloed across different systems.

## [Scenario C - Manufacturing Company](scenario-c.md)

A manufacturing company operating 3 factories wants to create a data product that **optimises production scheduling and reduces equipment downtime**. They have sensor data from machinery, production logs, quality control measurements, maintenance records, and supply chain data, but currently rely on manual reporting and Excel spreadsheets.

## [Scenario D - Financial Services](scenario-d.md)

A mid-sized bank wants to create a data product that **detects potentially fraudulent transactions in real-time** while minimising false positives that frustrate customers. They have transaction histories, customer profiles, merchant data, and historical fraud cases, but need to balance security with customer experience.
