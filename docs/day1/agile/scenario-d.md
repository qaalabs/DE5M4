# Breakout Activity: Scenario D - Financial Services

Apply your phases from the previous activity to the scenario below.

## Scenario

> A mid-sized bank wants to create a data product that **detects potentially fraudulent transactions in real-time** while minimising false positives that frustrate customers. They have transaction histories, customer profiles, merchant data, and historical fraud cases, but need to balance security with customer experience.

## Task

Using the design process your group created, outline how you would approach this specific project.

- What would be your first three phases?
- List 3 or 4 specific activities for each phase.
- What challenges do you anticipate?

## Group Sharing

As a group briefly present your **design process** and **initial approach** to the scenario. (2-3 minutes)
