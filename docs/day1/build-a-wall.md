# Opening Activity: How Long Will It Take to Build a Wall?

**Purpose:**
Get learners thinking about the uncertainty, assumptions, and dependencies behind estimation and planning.

---

## Step 1 – Set the scene (1 mins)

Say something like:

> “I want to build a wall outside my house. How long will it take?”

*Leave it hanging — don’t clarify yet. Display the question on screen or write it on a virtual whiteboard.*

## Step 2 – Individual quick estimate (2 mins)

Ask them to:

> “Write down a number — how long do you think it would take?”

Don’t let them ask questions yet. The goal is to capture gut instinct.

## Step 3 – Open up the questions (5–7 mins)

Then say:

> “What do you need to know before you can estimate confidently?”

Encourage them to shout out or add to a shared board:

* What kind of wall? (size, materials, design)
* Who’s building it? (you, team, builder)
* Tools or equipment?
* Weather conditions?
* Budget or deadline?
* Access to materials?
* Regulations or planning permission?

You’ll quickly get a wall (!) of questions - this is your pivot to “requirements and planning”.

## Step 4 – Compare the early estimates (3 mins)

Ask:

> “Now that we’ve talked through all those questions — who thinks their original estimate still holds?”

Most will laugh or admit it’s wrong.
That’s your moment to connect it to project planning:

> “Estimation depends on clarity, scope, and assumptions — and that’s true whether we’re building a wall or a digital product.”

## Step 5 – Discussion (7 mins)

Prompt discussion:

- What caused the biggest uncertainty?
- Which questions had the biggest impact on time/cost?
- What would help us *reduce* that uncertainty?
- How might this relate to designing software features or data systems?

## Step 6 - Connect to digital context (5 minutes)

**Transition:**

- What's our 'wall' in digital product work?

*Every feature, every user story is a little wall - and before we can plan it, we need to know enough about scope, design, and dependencies.*

You can open a short breakout or chat poll:

> What are examples of 'unknowns' that affect our estimates in tech projects?

*e.g. unclear requirements, data availability, integration complexity, etc.*

## Step 7 - Debrief and link to day's focus (5 minutes)

**Summarise:**

- You've just demonstrated why we need structure when estimating.
- This morning, we'll move from vague requests like 'build a wall' to clear, testable user stories.
- After lunch, we'll see how teams use tools like Planning Poker to manage this uncertainty together.

---

### Facilitator Tips

- Use humour and curiosity - not too serious.
- Keep the energy high with quick pacing.
- Resist the urge to explain too soon; let learners experience ambiguity first.
- Use a shared board or chat to make the "what we need to know" list visible.
