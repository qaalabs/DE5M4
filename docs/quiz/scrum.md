## Which of the following are the essential pillars of the Scrum empirical process? (Choose 3 answers)

- [ ] Tools
- [X] Transparency
- [X] Inspection
- [X] Adaptation

If we look at the scrum events, we can see these three pillars at work.  
For example, the scrum review and daily scrum meetings offer transparency to team members, collaborators, and stakeholders.  
And, the scrum review and retrospective meetings offer opportunities for both inspection and adaptation.

## During the daily scrum meeting, a team member raises an issue that is blocking progress of a work item.  What should happen next?

- [ ] The team should use the remaining time during the scrum meeting to find a solution for the blocked work item.
- [X] The team members impacted by the blocked work item should meet after the scrum to discuss solutions.
- [ ] The scrum master will decide whether or not to extend the sprint to complete the work.
- [ ] The work item should be removed from the sprint until a solution is identified, and the developer should take on another task.

If issues or questions arise in the daily scrum meeting that cannot be addressed easily, the team should set aside time outside the scrum meeting to discuss the issue.  
The scrum master can help facilitate a meeting if needed and help reach out to collaborators or stakeholders.  
It is not a best practice to change the sprint's length or remove work items from a sprint.

## Which of the following activities is the Scrum Master's responsibility? (Choose 2 answers)

- [X] guiding the team to help them understand and practice scrum methodologies
- [ ] managing the product backlog by prioritizing work items
- [X] facilitating scrum events and communication
- [ ] managing the sprint backlog by assigning work to the development team

One of the Scrum Master's primary responsibilities is to be a servant leader that helps and guides the team to understand and practice good scrum methodologies.
Part of this servant leadership is to help the team remove obstacles that impede work progress by facilitating communication with collaborators and stakeholders.
The Scrum Master also is responsible for facilitating scrum events.

For more information on the scrum master role and responsibilities, look at this [Scrum Team](https://www.scrumalliance.org/about-scrum/team) reference page.

## Which of the following sprint events is used to show / demonstrate sprint results to collaborators and stakeholders?

- [ ] The sprint review meeting
- [ ] The daily scrum (stand-up) meeting
- [ ] The sprint planning meeting
- [ ] The sprint retrospective meeting

A sprint review is an event that occurs at the end of a sprint and is a time when the development team presents the product work completed during the sprint to collaborators and stakeholders.  
During the review, the stakeholders often provide feedback, and this information is incorporated into the product backlog as needed.

For more information on scrum events, take a look at this [Scrum Events](https://www.scrumalliance.org/about-scrum/events) reference.

## Which of the following options would be considered good characteristics of a well-defined user story? (Choose 3 answers) 

- [X] small
- [X] negotiable
- [X] independent
- [ ] prescriptive

You can use the INVEST mnemonic to help remember the key characteristics of a well-written product backlog work item (user story).

A user story should be...

**Independent:**
- The work item should be self-contained and should not be interconnected with other work items.

**Negotiable:**
- The work item should not be overly prescriptive; it should leave room for creative discussion.

**Valuable:**
- The work should provide value to the team, collaborators, or stakeholders.

**Estimable:**
- The work item should contain enough information that the team can estimate its relative work effort.

**Small:**
- The work item should be small enough to fit within a sprint.  Ideally, work items should be as small as possible and still provide value.

**Testable:**
The work item should include information in the form of acceptance criteria to be able to test and verify when the work item is complete.
