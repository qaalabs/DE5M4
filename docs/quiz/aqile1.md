# Stacey's process complexity model shows us that Agile thinking is best _______________. (Choose two answers)

- [X] When there is less understanding of the requirements or the solution
- [ ] When there is high certainty
- [X] When there is high complexity
- [ ] When there is low certainty and low complexity

According to the Stacey’s process complexity model, an Agile approach is best suited for high uncertainty/complexity. 
A low understanding of requirements or the solution leads to high uncertainty and potentially high complexity.

Adopting an Agile mindset is beneficial for all circumstances.
